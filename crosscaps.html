TACTICS & RULES

Mastering the Strategic Arena

1
Interactive Die: Tap the center 3D die 🎲 on the laser line to roll!
2
Launching Caps: Tap the caps in your zone, then tap LAUNCH CAPS ⚡ or tap the glowing center die!
3
Tactical Abilities (1 use each per round):
• 🎲 Overclock: Choose your exact roll!
• 💥 Vaporize: Directly eliminate 1 of your own caps!
• 🎰 Shaker: Reroll if you get an unfavorable number.
4
Special Caps:
• 💣 Blast Cap: Explodes on impact and vaporizes 1 enemy cap!
• ⚡ Turbo Cap: Awards an immediate bonus turn!
• 🌟 Wild Cap: Triggers bonus sabotage!

6
⏱️ Sudden Death (Turn 16): If neither player clears by Turn 16, the player with the fewest own caps in their arena wins by judgment!
